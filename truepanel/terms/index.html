2. What the app is

TruePanel works out how to cut a list of parts from sheet material, draws the cut plan to scale, guides you through the cuts, prints part labels and prepares quotes.

3. What the app is not

  • Not a substitute for measuring. Cut plans are calculated carefully, but they are only as good as the numbers you enter. Real sheets are not always their nominal size, edges are not always square and blades differ in kerf. Check the sheet, the settings and the first part before you cut the rest.
  • Not a guarantee. A layout that fits on screen is not a promise that the material will yield it.

4. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for verifying every measurement before cutting, and for the material you cut: wasted or damaged material is your responsibility. Follow the safety rules for your saw. Do not look at the phone while a blade is running.

5. Free and Pro

Free, with no account and no ads: up to 3 projects, up to 40 parts per project and one sheet material per plan, with the full drawn cut plan and its PDF. Template previews are free.

TruePanel Pro removes those limits and opens the templates in full. The current list of Pro features is shown in the app before you buy.
