Privacy Policy

The short version. TruePanel has no account, no advertising and no tracking across other apps, and we sell no data. Your projects, parts and cut plans stay on your phone. Three things leave the device: a TruePanel Pro purchase, handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat; and anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which you can turn off with one switch — never your projects or measurements.

1. Who we are

TruePanel ("the app") is made by heyisoft, a sole developer based in İzmir, Türkiye. You can reach us at heyisoft@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The app stores the following in a database on the device:

  • your projects, parts, sheet materials and the cut plans calculated from them;
  • your business details for quotes, including a logo you choose;
  • your unit, language, theme and other settings.

None of this is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no backup on our side, which also means we cannot restore it for you — use the app's own backup instead (section 5).

3. Permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition — only in cut mode, when you turn on voice commands (next, back, repeat, done). Recognition runs on the device; no audio is recorded, stored or sent to us.
  • Bluetooth and local network — only to find and print to your label printer. On older Android versions (10 and below) the system calls the Bluetooth scan permission "location"; the app does not read your location.
  • Files and photos — only what you pick yourself: a logo for your quotes, a CSV or Excel part list to import, or a backup file to restore. The chosen file is read on the device.

The app does not use the camera, your location or your contacts.

4. Purchases

TruePanel Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription. When you buy either of them:

  • Apple or Google takes the payment and holds the billing details. We never see your card, your address or your name.
  • RevenueCat (our purchase provider) receives the store receipt and an anonymous app user ID that the SDK generates on the device. It uses them to tell the app whether TruePanel Pro is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

5. Backup, sharing and printing

A backup, a cut plan, a quote or a label sheet is created on the device and handed to your operating system's share sheet or printer. It goes wherever you send it — e-mail, a cloud drive, another app. The app does not upload it to us.

6. Usage statistics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used, so we know what is worth improving. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage and crash reports. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

What is recorded is the shape of the session, never its content:

  • that the app was opened, onboarding was completed, a project was created, a plan was solved or exported, or cut mode was started;
  • which template was opened, customized or turned into a project, and rough counts in ranges (for example how many parts or sheets a plan has, or how many offcuts it reused) — never the dimensions;
  • which features were used, by kind only: the kind of export (PDF, CSV, Excel, DXF, image, labels), the label printer's command language and connection type (Bluetooth or network), voice cut mode started or ended, cut mode finished, a part operation added (miter, dado or hole), a sheet defect added, a customer or quote created, a backup shared or restored and whether it worked, a parts import and whether it worked, a sheet or lumber project chosen, the plan goal, an offcut kept, a material switched, a kerf changed, and that the app asked for a store rating;
  • unit setting;
  • that the TruePanel Pro screen was shown, why, which plan was chosen, and whether a purchase or restore started, completed, was cancelled or failed;
  • an app instance identifier generated on the device, plus the standard technical fields Firebase collects: device model, operating system version, country (derived from IP) and app version.

It does not record your measurements, project or part names, your business details, your name, your e-mail address or your location.

7. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: the technical stack trace, device model, operating system and app version, and the same kind of app instance identifier. It contains no project data, and the app adds no custom fields or user identifier to it. We use it only to fix the bug. The same switch, Settings → Share anonymous usage and crash reports, turns crash reports off; when you turn it off, reports not yet sent are deleted from the device.

8. What we never do

There is no advertising and no advertising SDK. We do not sell or rent data, share it with data brokers, or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

9. Children

TruePanel is a workshop tool. It is not directed at children, and the statistics described above contain no personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, the Turkish KVKK and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data. In practice: your projects are on your own device, so you already have all of it, and uninstalling erases it. For purchase records, contact Apple, Google or RevenueCat — they are the holders. For usage statistics and crash reports, write to us and we will delete the data tied to your app instance identifier.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published here before the app version that needs it ships.
