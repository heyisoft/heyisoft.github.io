Every part on the sheet, before the saw starts.

TruePanel lays out your cabinet and furniture parts on plywood and MDF sheets, draws the cut plan to scale, and walks you through it cut by cut — hands-free if you want. Everything is worked out on your phone.

A drawn cut plan

Parts nested on each sheet with kerf, grain and edge banding taken into account, so you can see the waste before you buy.

Templates

Base and wall cabinets, wardrobes, bookcases, desks, beds and more. Change the size and the part list follows.

Cut mode

One cut at a time on a big screen. Say next, back, repeat or done; speech is recognised on the device.

Labels and quotes

Print part labels on a Bluetooth or network label printer, and turn a plan into a quote with your own logo.

Import a part list

Bring parts in from a CSV or Excel file instead of typing them again.

No account

Projects are stored on your phone. Back them up to a file whenever you like.
