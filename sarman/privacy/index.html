Privacy Policy

🐾 The short version. Sarman has no accounts. Your cat's recordings, diary, chat, care records (meals, litter box, vaccines, medication, weight, vet visits, expenses, files) and notes stay on your phone — the sound analysis runs entirely on the device and no audio is ever uploaded. Two features send something off your phone, and only when you use them: Ask AI sends your question and a short profile of your cat, and Dress up sends the one photo you pick. Both go to our server and to Google's Gemini API, which writes the answer or creates the costume picture. We do not keep your questions or photos. Apart from that, only anonymous usage and crash data and purchase records leave your phone. Reminders (care, vaccines, medication and the others below) are created and shown by your phone itself; nothing about them is sent to us. PDFs (vet summary, lost-cat poster) are made on your phone and leave it only if you share them. When you run out of free uses you may choose to watch an optional rewarded video ad, served by Google AdMob; AdMob can use your device's advertising identifier, and on iPhone it only does so if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt. Subscribers see no ads.

4. What we do not collect

  • No audio ever leaves your phone. The sound model runs on the device; there is no server to send it to.
  • No notes, no diary content, no care records. Your cat's name and short profile are sent only with an Ask AI question you send. No photos, except the single photo you send each time you choose to use Dress up.
  • No account, e-mail address, name or phone number — the app has no sign-up.
  • No contacts, files or precise location. Before a Dress up photo is sent to Google, our server re-encodes it, which removes embedded metadata such as GPS location and camera details.
  • We do not read your advertising identifier ourselves and we do not build advertising profiles. Advertising data is collected only by Google AdMob when an ad is loaded or shown, as described in section 8, and never for subscribers.
  • No card details — purchases are paid to Apple or Google, and we never receive your payment data.

5. How the sound analysis works

When you record your cat, the app measures acoustic properties of the sound — duration, pitch, how the pitch moves, roughness, harmonic structure — and compares them against a model that ships inside the app. All of this happens on your phone, offline. Nothing is streamed, queued or uploaded. If you delete a recording, it is gone.

Sarman is not a veterinary device and does not diagnose. Its "health radar" only points out when your cat's own pattern shifts from its usual; Ask AI answers are general information, not veterinary advice; the vaccine schedule is a general suggestion to check with your vet. If something feels off with your cat, talk to your vet.

5a. Ask AI: questions about your cat's behaviour

Ask AI lets you ask a question about your cat in the chat. Nothing is sent until you type a question and tap send. Before your first question, the app tells you what is sent and where, and asks you to agree.

  • What is sent. Your question, your app language, the short cat profile and meow-mood counts listed in section 3, the anonymous install identifier and an App Check token. For the weekly AI summary, the app writes the request itself from the counts listed in section 3. No recordings, photos, note texts, medication names or vet contacts are sent.
  • Our server (ai.cockit.io), operated by heyisoft. It checks that the request comes from the genuine app (Firebase App Check), checks your subscription with RevenueCat, counts your questions against your allowance, and forwards the text to Google. The question and the answer are handled in memory only: they are not written to disk, not kept, and not logged. Our logs record only technical facts such as a shortened hash of the install identifier, the result status, the number of AI tokens used and the processing time. Your install identifier is kept only as a salted one-way hash, next to the number of questions you have used (free users: the one free question; Sarman Pro subscribers: the allowance of 100 questions a month).
  • Google Gemini API. The text is processed by Google LLC as our data processor under the paid-service terms described in section 6 for Dress up: Google does not use it to improve its products, and may keep it for up to 55 days only for abuse monitoring.
  • Your choice. Ask AI is optional. You can withdraw your agreement at any time with Sending questions to AI in Settings; after that nothing is sent until you agree again. Answers already in your chat stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Reporting. Tap Report under any AI answer to tell us it was offensive, wrong or unsafe. Only the reason you picked reaches us (as an anonymous usage event); the question and the answer are not sent. We use reports to improve the instructions and filters of the AI.

6. Dress up: AI costume photos

Dress up is the only part of Sarman that sends a photo off your phone, and it only does so when you ask for it. Nothing is uploaded until you have chosen a photo and a costume and tapped Dress up. Before your first photo is sent, the app tells you where it goes and asks you to agree.

  • What is sent. The one photo you chose, reduced in size on your phone, the costume you picked, the anonymous install identifier and an App Check token. The app cannot send any text or instructions of its own: the costume description is fixed on our server.
  • Our server (ai.cockit.io), operated by heyisoft. It checks that the request comes from the genuine app (Firebase App Check, which uses Apple's App Attest on iOS and Google Play Integrity on Android), checks your subscription and tokens with RevenueCat, re-encodes the photo to strip metadata, and forwards it to Google. The photo and the result are handled in memory only: they are not written to disk, not kept, and not logged. Our logs record only technical facts such as the costume, the result status and the processing time. Your install identifier is kept only as a salted one-way hash, next to the number of tokens you have used. Your IP address is used to limit abuse; our application does not store it together with your photo.
  • Google Gemini API. The photo and the costume description are processed by Google LLC as our data processor, under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service for paid services. Under those terms Google does not use the photo or the result to improve its products. Google logs prompts and responses for a limited period, solely to detect and prevent violations of its Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy and for legally required disclosures; Google's abuse-monitoring documentation states this period as 55 days. This data may be stored transiently or cached in any country where Google or its agents have facilities. Before a costume is made, a Gemini model first checks that the photo shows a real cat and no person; a photo in which a person is visible is refused. Google's automated safety filters may also refuse a photo or a result. In all of these cases no token is used.
  • The result. The costume picture is returned to your phone and stored in the app on your device. It is saved to your photo library or shared only when you tap Save or Share. Pictures created by Gemini carry Google's invisible SynthID watermark, which identifies them as AI-generated.
  • Your choice. Dress up is optional. If you never use it, no photo ever leaves your phone, and the rest of the app works exactly the same. Please only use photos of your own cat, and do not send photos showing people.
  • Reporting. If a created picture is offensive or wrong, use Report on that picture in the app, or write to heyisoft@gmail.com. A report sends us the reason you chose, the costume and the anonymous install identifier; the picture itself is not sent. We keep a report for up to 30 days to review it, then delete it. We use reports to improve our filters.

Every service provider named in this policy — Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and App Check, the Gemini API for Ask AI and Dress up, and AdMob) and RevenueCat — receives only the data needed for the task described here, processes it under its own published terms, and is required by those terms to protect it at least as well as this policy describes.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

To keep improving the app we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They record anonymous events — for example that an analysis was attempted and whether it succeeded — together with basic technical data such as device model, operating-system version and app version. This is used in aggregate; it is not tied to your name or an account (there is none), it is never sold, and it never includes your cat's name, its sounds, your photos, your notes, or the content of your questions, answers and care records. Collection of the advertising ID is switched off in both services. Both services are covered by Firebase's privacy terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Advertising

Sarman has one kind of ad: an optional rewarded video. When you run out of free listens or chat phrases, you can choose to watch a video to the end to get 3 more of the one you ran out of for that day, up to 3 videos a day. There are no banners, no interstitials and no ads at app launch. Subscribers see no ads, and no ad is loaded for them.

Videos are served by Google AdMob, which is part of Google LLC. When an ad is requested, the AdMob SDK in the app collects and sends to Google the data listed in section 3: device identifiers, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Google uses it to serve and measure the ad, to prevent fraud and, if you agree, to personalise the ad, and handles it as described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ad partners may use the advertising identifier to show you ads and to measure them, which can include linking activity across apps. The AdMob SDK also reads technical information from your device when it loads an ad; it does not read your recordings, photos, notes or cat profiles. Sarman is not directed at children and does not knowingly show ads to them.

  • Your advertising identifier. Android Advertising ID and Apple IDFA can be used for advertising. You can reset it or switch off personalised ads in your system settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • iPhone and iPad. Right after the app first opens (before any ad can load), Sarman shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt once. Only if you choose Allow can the IDFA be used and your activity be tracked across other companies' apps and websites for advertising. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you still get the reward, but the ad is not personalised and the IDFA is not available. Sarman does not track you in any other way, and not at all unless you allow it.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland. Before the first ad request, Google's consent form (built with Google's User Messaging Platform) asks whether ads may use your data for personalisation. If you decline, you can still watch a video, but it is a non-personalised ad. Where this form applies, you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.
  • Android. The app declares the permission to read the Android Advertising ID. If you have reset the ID or switched off ad personalisation, ads are non-personalised and the ID may be unavailable.
  • Dress up and ads. Photos sent for Dress up are never shared with AdMob or any ad network.

9. Purchases, subscriptions and tokens

There is one optional subscription, Sarman Pro, with a weekly plan (US$1.99 per week) and a monthly plan (US$5.99 per month), listed in US dollars, varying by region and shown in your own currency in the app. A yearly plan is no longer offered to new subscribers; existing yearly subscriptions continue. It removes the daily free limits on listening and chat phrases, opens the health radar, and adds Ask AI (up to 100 questions a month; everyone gets 1 free question), the weekly AI summary and the vet summary and lost-cat poster PDFs, and removes ads. The everyday Care tools are free. If you are eligible, the monthly plan starts with a 3-day free trial (the weekly plan has none); the store decides eligibility. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts and you are charged. You can also buy a one-time pack of 20 Dress up tokens (US$6.99, shown in your own currency in the app); Dress up is not part of Sarman Pro. The terms of both are in our Terms of Use. Payment is taken by Apple or Google, never by us: we never see your card, your billing address or your name.

We use RevenueCat to check that a subscription or token purchase is valid and to restore a subscription when you reinstall or change phone. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt — product, purchase and renewal dates, store country — together with an anonymous identifier the app generates for your install. Our Ask AI and Dress up server asks RevenueCat, using that identifier, whether you have an active subscription and how many tokens you have bought. The identifier is not tied to your name or an e-mail address, because the app has neither. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. The counters for your free listens and free phrases (which reset every day at midnight in your device's time), and for the rewarded videos you watched today, are kept on your device only.

Trial reminder. When a free trial starts, the app asks for permission to send notifications. If you agree, it schedules one notification on your device about 24 hours before the trial ends. This is a local notification: it is created and shown by your phone, and no data about it is sent to us or anyone else. If you decline, the trial and the app work the same; you just get no reminder. This reminder does not depend on the Notifications setting below.

Care reminders. If you turn them on, the app schedules local notifications for the care reminders you set (for example a medication at 20:00), for upcoming vaccines (the day before, at 10:00) and when a medication is running low. Their text can include your cat's name and the reminder or medication name you typed; it is read on your device only and never sent to us or anyone else.

Optional reminders. Sarman can also send local notifications: a reminder at about 10:00 your time the day after you used your free uses (for people without a subscription), and a reminder about 3 and 7 days after you last opened the app. The app asks first, in its own screen: during setup, when you choose care reminders, or after your free uses run out or after your first successful chat; if you answer Not now it may ask once more, about 3 days later, and never again. You can switch them off at any time in Settings. They are scheduled and shown by your phone. The text can include your cat's name, which is read on your device only and is never sent to us or anyone else. Tapping a reminder just opens the app.

10. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone — required for recording. It is the whole point of the app: recording your cat's meow. Audio is analysed locally and never uploaded.
  • Notifications — optional. Asked for when a free trial starts (to remind you about 24 hours before it ends), when you choose care reminders, or when you agree to reminders about your free daily uses and about coming back to the app. They are created on your phone; you can switch them off in Settings or in your system settings.
  • Camera — optional. To take a profile photo of your cat, a photo to dress up, or a photo for a care record.
  • Photos and files — optional. To pick a photo of your cat for the profile, for Dress up or for a care record, or a document (for example a lab result) to keep in Care (through the system photo picker, which shares only the photo you select), and, if you tap Save, to add a costume picture to your library.

You can refuse or withdraw any of these in your system settings. Without the microphone the analysis cannot run, but the rest of the app still works.

11. Device backups

Because your data lives on the device, it may be included in the backup you control (Google One / iCloud), encrypted by Google or Apple. We never receive that backup. On Android we go a step further and exclude sound recordings, photos and the files you attach in Care from cloud backup (your care records themselves are included, so they survive a phone change); they are still carried over on a direct phone-to-phone transfer, which does not pass through anyone's cloud.

12. Legal bases (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Performance of contract — running the app you installed, processing recordings on your device to give you the result you asked for, and providing the subscription and tokens you bought.
  • Consent — sending your Ask AI question and your cat's short profile to our server and to Google's Gemini API. You give it in the app before your first question, and you can withdraw it at any time with Sending questions to AI in Settings.
  • Consent — sending a photo you chose to our server and to Google's Gemini API for Dress up. You give it in the app before your first photo is sent, and you can withdraw it at any time by turning off Dress up photo sharing in Settings → Your data, or simply by not using Dress up; withdrawing does not affect pictures already created.
  • Consent (advertising) — personalised ads and the use of your advertising identifier for them, through Google's consent form (Europe, UK, Switzerland) or Apple's tracking prompt (iOS). Watching a rewarded video is optional, and you can change the choice as described in section 8. Without consent you still get the reward, with non-personalised ads.
  • Legitimate interests — anonymous analytics and crash reporting to keep the app working and improve it, and preventing abuse of the Ask AI and Dress up server.

13. Retention

  • Everything on your device, including Dress up pictures, stays until you delete it (per item, or all at once with Settings → Your data → Erase all my data) or until you uninstall the app.
  • Ask AI questions and answers: not stored on our server; the answer is kept in your chat on your device. Google may keep the text for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring. Question counters (salted hash of the install identifier and the number of questions used) are kept for as long as the allowance applies: the free question for as long as the app is installed, the monthly count until shortly after the month ends.
  • Dress up photos and results: not stored on our server. Google may keep them for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring, as described in section 6. Reports are deleted after at most 30 days.
  • Dress up token counters (salted hash of the install identifier and the number of tokens used): counters for the monthly allowance of subscribers who subscribed before version 1.5.0 expire shortly after each billing period ends; counters for bought tokens are kept for as long as those tokens can be used, because bought tokens do not expire.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Google under Firebase's own retention settings.
  • Ad data collected by AdMob is held by Google under its own policies; we do not receive or keep it. The daily count of videos you watched, your reminder choice and the planned reminder times stay on your device.

14. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and Turkish KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because essentially everything lives on your device, the most direct tool is the in-app Erase all my data action, which removes profiles, diary, chat, care records, reminders, personal labels, recordings, photos and attached files for good. For anything else, write to heyisoft@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

15. Children

Sarman is for users aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process children's data; if you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we will delete any data we hold about it. Please do not send photos that show people to Dress up, and never photos of children. Do not ask Ask AI about people.
