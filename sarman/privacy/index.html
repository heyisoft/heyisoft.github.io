Privacy Policy

🐾 The short version. Sarman has no accounts. Your cat's recordings, diary and notes stay on your phone — the sound analysis runs entirely on the device and no audio is ever uploaded. Photos stay on your phone too, with one exception you control: when you use Dress up, the one photo you pick is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini API to create the costume picture. We do not keep that photo. Apart from that, only anonymous usage and crash data and purchase records leave your phone. Optional reminder notifications are created and shown by your phone itself; nothing about them is sent to us. When you run out of free uses you may choose to watch an optional rewarded video ad, served by Google AdMob; AdMob can use your device's advertising identifier, and on iPhone it only does so if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt. Subscribers see no ads.

1. Who we are

Sarman ("the app") is developed and published by Hüseyin İris, an independent developer publishing as heyisoft ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the app. For any privacy matter, write to heyisoft@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Sarman mobile application for iOS and Android, the server the app uses for the Dress up feature, and this website. It explains what the app handles, where it is processed, and the choices you have.

4. What we do not collect

  • No audio ever leaves your phone. The sound model runs on the device; there is no server to send it to.
  • No notes, no cat names, no diary content. No photos, except the single photo you send each time you choose to use Dress up.
  • No account, e-mail address, name or phone number — the app has no sign-up.
  • No contacts, files or precise location. Before a Dress up photo is sent to Google, our server re-encodes it, which removes embedded metadata such as GPS location and camera details.
  • We do not read your advertising identifier ourselves and we do not build advertising profiles. Advertising data is collected only by Google AdMob when an ad is loaded or shown, as described in section 8, and never for subscribers.
  • No card details — purchases are paid to Apple or Google, and we never receive your payment data.

5. How the sound analysis works

When you record your cat, the app measures acoustic properties of the sound — duration, pitch, how the pitch moves, roughness, harmonic structure — and compares them against a model that ships inside the app. All of this happens on your phone, offline. Nothing is streamed, queued or uploaded. If you delete a recording, it is gone.

Sarman is not a veterinary device and does not diagnose. Its "health radar" only points out when your cat's own pattern shifts from its usual. If something feels off with your cat, talk to your vet.

6. Dress up: AI costume photos

Dress up is the only part of Sarman that sends a photo off your phone, and it only does so when you ask for it. Nothing is uploaded until you have chosen a photo and a costume and tapped Dress up. Before your first photo is sent, the app tells you where it goes and asks you to agree.

  • What is sent. The one photo you chose, reduced in size on your phone, the costume you picked, the anonymous install identifier and an App Check token. The app cannot send any text or instructions of its own: the costume description is fixed on our server.
  • Our server (ai.cockit.io), operated by heyisoft. It checks that the request comes from the genuine app (Firebase App Check, which uses Apple's App Attest on iOS and Google Play Integrity on Android), checks your subscription and tokens with RevenueCat, re-encodes the photo to strip metadata, and forwards it to Google. The photo and the result are handled in memory only: they are not written to disk, not kept, and not logged. Our logs record only technical facts such as the costume, the result status and the processing time. Your install identifier is kept only as a salted one-way hash, next to the number of tokens you have used. Your IP address is used to limit abuse; our application does not store it together with your photo.
  • Google Gemini API. The photo and the costume description are processed by Google LLC as our data processor, under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service for paid services. Under those terms Google does not use the photo or the result to improve its products. Google logs prompts and responses for a limited period, solely to detect and prevent violations of its Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy and for legally required disclosures; Google's abuse-monitoring documentation states this period as 55 days. This data may be stored transiently or cached in any country where Google or its agents have facilities. Before a costume is made, a Gemini model first checks that the photo shows a real cat and no person; a photo in which a person is visible is refused. Google's automated safety filters may also refuse a photo or a result. In all of these cases no token is used.
  • The result. The costume picture is returned to your phone and stored in the app on your device. It is saved to your photo library or shared only when you tap Save or Share. Pictures created by Gemini carry Google's invisible SynthID watermark, which identifies them as AI-generated.
  • Your choice. Dress up is optional. If you never use it, no photo ever leaves your phone, and the rest of the app works exactly the same. Please only use photos of your own cat, and do not send photos showing people.
  • Reporting. If a created picture is offensive or wrong, use Report on that picture in the app, or write to heyisoft@gmail.com. A report sends us the reason you chose, the costume and the anonymous install identifier; the picture itself is not sent. We keep a report for up to 30 days to review it, then delete it. We use reports to improve our filters.

Every service provider named in this policy — Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and App Check, the Gemini API and AdMob) and RevenueCat — receives only the data needed for the task described here, processes it under its own published terms, and is required by those terms to protect it at least as well as this policy describes.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

To keep improving the app we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They record anonymous events — for example that an analysis was attempted and whether it succeeded — together with basic technical data such as device model, operating-system version and app version. This is used in aggregate; it is not tied to your name or an account (there is none), it is never sold, and it never includes your cat's name, its sounds, your photos or your notes. Collection of the advertising ID is switched off in both services. Both services are covered by Firebase's privacy terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Advertising

Sarman has one kind of ad: an optional rewarded video. When you run out of free listens or chat phrases, you can choose to watch a video to the end to get 3 more of the one you ran out of for that day, up to 3 videos a day. There are no banners, no interstitials and no ads at app launch. Subscribers see no ads, and no ad is loaded for them.

Videos are served by Google AdMob, which is part of Google LLC. When an ad is requested, the AdMob SDK in the app collects and sends to Google the data listed in section 3: device identifiers, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Google uses it to serve and measure the ad, to prevent fraud and, if you agree, to personalise the ad, and handles it as described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ad partners may use the advertising identifier to show you ads and to measure them, which can include linking activity across apps. The AdMob SDK also reads technical information from your device when it loads an ad; it does not read your recordings, photos, notes or cat profiles. Sarman is not directed at children and does not knowingly show ads to them.

  • Your advertising identifier. Android Advertising ID and Apple IDFA can be used for advertising. You can reset it or switch off personalised ads in your system settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • iPhone and iPad. The first time you tap to watch a video, Sarman shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (never when the app opens). Only if you choose Allow can the IDFA be used and your activity be tracked across other companies' apps and websites for advertising. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you still get the reward, but the ad is not personalised and the IDFA is not available. Sarman does not track you in any other way, and not at all unless you allow it.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland. Before the first ad request, Google's consent form (built with Google's User Messaging Platform) asks whether ads may use your data for personalisation. If you decline, you can still watch a video, but it is a non-personalised ad. Where this form applies, you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.
  • Android. The app declares the permission to read the Android Advertising ID. If you have reset the ID or switched off ad personalisation, ads are non-personalised and the ID may be unavailable.
  • Dress up and ads. Photos sent for Dress up are never shared with AdMob or any ad network.

9. Purchases, subscriptions and tokens

There is one optional subscription, Sarman Pro, with a weekly plan (US$1.99 per week) and a monthly plan (US$5.99 per month), listed in US dollars, varying by region and shown in your own currency in the app. A yearly plan is no longer offered to new subscribers; existing yearly subscriptions continue. It removes the daily free limits on listening and chat phrases, opens the health radar and the vet summary, and removes ads. If you are eligible, the monthly plan starts with a 3-day free trial (the weekly plan has none); the store decides eligibility. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts and you are charged. You can also buy a one-time pack of 20 Dress up tokens (US$6.99, shown in your own currency in the app); Dress up is not part of Sarman Pro. The terms of both are in our Terms of Use. Payment is taken by Apple or Google, never by us: we never see your card, your billing address or your name.

We use RevenueCat to check that a subscription or token purchase is valid and to restore a subscription when you reinstall or change phone. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt — product, purchase and renewal dates, store country — together with an anonymous identifier the app generates for your install. Our Dress up server asks RevenueCat, using that identifier, whether you have an active subscription and how many tokens you have bought. The identifier is not tied to your name or an e-mail address, because the app has neither. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. The counters for your free listens and free phrases (which reset every day at midnight in your device's time), and for the rewarded videos you watched today, are kept on your device only.

Trial reminder. When a free trial starts, the app asks for permission to send notifications. If you agree, it schedules one notification on your device about 24 hours before the trial ends. This is a local notification: it is created and shown by your phone, and no data about it is sent to us or anyone else. If you decline, the trial and the app work the same; you just get no reminder. This reminder does not depend on the Notifications setting below.

Optional reminders. Sarman can also send local notifications: a reminder at about 10:00 your time the day after you used your free uses (for people without a subscription), and a reminder about 3 and 7 days after you last opened the app. The app asks first, in its own screen, after your free uses run out or after your first successful chat; if you answer Not now it may ask once more, about 3 days later, and never again. You can switch them off at any time in Settings. They are scheduled and shown by your phone. The text can include your cat's name, which is read on your device only and is never sent to us or anyone else. Tapping a reminder just opens the app.

10. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone — required for recording. It is the whole point of the app: recording your cat's meow. Audio is analysed locally and never uploaded.
  • Notifications — optional. Asked for when a free trial starts (to remind you about 24 hours before it ends) or when you agree to reminders about your free daily uses and about coming back to the app. They are created on your phone; you can switch them off in Settings or in your system settings.
  • Camera — optional. To take a profile photo of your cat, or a photo to dress up.
  • Photos — optional. To pick a photo of your cat for the profile or for Dress up (through the system photo picker, which shares only the photo you select), and, if you tap Save, to add a costume picture to your library.

You can refuse or withdraw any of these in your system settings. Without the microphone the analysis cannot run, but the rest of the app still works.

11. Device backups

Because your data lives on the device, it may be included in the backup you control (Google One / iCloud), encrypted by Google or Apple. We never receive that backup. On Android we go a step further and exclude sound recordings and photos from cloud backup; they are still carried over on a direct phone-to-phone transfer, which does not pass through anyone's cloud.

12. Legal bases (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Performance of contract — running the app you installed, processing recordings on your device to give you the result you asked for, and providing the subscription and tokens you bought.
  • Consent — sending a photo you chose to our server and to Google's Gemini API for Dress up. You give it in the app before your first photo is sent, and you can withdraw it at any time by turning off Dress up photo sharing in Settings → Your data, or simply by not using Dress up; withdrawing does not affect pictures already created.
  • Consent (advertising) — personalised ads and the use of your advertising identifier for them, through Google's consent form (Europe, UK, Switzerland) or Apple's tracking prompt (iOS). Watching a rewarded video is optional, and you can change the choice as described in section 8. Without consent you still get the reward, with non-personalised ads.
  • Legitimate interests — anonymous analytics and crash reporting to keep the app working and improve it, and preventing abuse of the Dress up server.

13. Retention

  • Everything on your device, including Dress up pictures, stays until you delete it (per item, or all at once with Settings → Your data → Erase all my data) or until you uninstall the app.
  • Dress up photos and results: not stored on our server. Google may keep them for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring, as described in section 6. Reports are deleted after at most 30 days.
  • Dress up token counters (salted hash of the install identifier and the number of tokens used): counters for the monthly allowance of subscribers who subscribed before version 1.5.0 expire shortly after each billing period ends; counters for bought tokens are kept for as long as those tokens can be used, because bought tokens do not expire.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Google under Firebase's own retention settings.
  • Ad data collected by AdMob is held by Google under its own policies; we do not receive or keep it. The daily count of videos you watched, your reminder choice and the planned reminder times stay on your device.

14. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and Turkish KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because essentially everything lives on your device, the most direct tool is the in-app Erase all my data action, which removes profiles, diary, chat, personal labels, recordings and photos for good. For anything else, write to heyisoft@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

We do not knowingly process children's data; if you believe a child has used the app, write to us and we will delete any data we hold about it. Please do not send photos that show people to Dress up, and never photos of children.
