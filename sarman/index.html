Sarman

A cat translator that runs entirely on your phone. Record a meow, see what changed, keep a diary — and talk back with real recorded cat calls. No ads, no subscription, and no audio ever leaves your device.

Listen

Record a meow and get the read: mood, confidence, pitch, roughness. When it isn't sure, it says so instead of guessing.

Talk back

Tap a phrase and your cat hears a real recorded cat call matched to that intent — a greeting chirrup, an insistent food meow.

Diary

Every meow you keep becomes an entry. Over days you get a picture instead of a single guess.

Notices drift

Sarman learns your cat's normal and quietly flags when it shifts. It never names an illness and never diagnoses.

Learns your cat

Label a few of their sounds and the read sharpens for that individual. Cats differ; a general model only goes so far.

Stays on your phone

Recordings, photos and the diary never leave the device. One tap in Settings erases everything, for real.

🐾 Honest about what this is. Nobody can turn a meow into a sentence, and any app promising that is selling you something. What can be done is measuring the acoustics of a meow and mapping them onto contexts research has actually recorded. Sarman shows its confidence and abstains when the signal is weak. It is not a veterinary device and does not diagnose.
