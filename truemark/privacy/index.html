Privacy Policy

The short version. Truemark has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your settings, your bender values and your saved jobs stay on your phone. The only thing that ever leaves the device is a subscription purchase, and that is handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat — never by us directly.

1. Who we are

Truemark ("the app") is made by heyisoft, a sole developer based in İzmir, Türkiye. You can reach us at huseyiniriss@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

All of it. The app stores the following locally, using the operating system's own preference storage:

  • your unit, precision, language, theme and motion settings;
  • your bender model and any take-up or radius you measured yourself;
  • jobs you saved, including the measurements you typed and which bender was selected at the time.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no backup on our side, which also means we cannot restore it for you.

3. Sensors

The digital level reads the phone's accelerometer. The reading is used to draw the angle on screen and is never stored or transmitted. The app does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts or your photos.

4. Subscriptions

Truemark Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. When you subscribe:

  • Apple or Google takes the payment and holds the billing details. We never see your card, your address or your name.
  • RevenueCat (our subscription provider) receives the store receipt and an anonymous app user ID that the SDK generates on the device. It uses them to tell the app whether the subscription is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That anonymous ID is not linked to an account, an e-mail address or an advertising identifier, because the app has none of those.

5. Sharing and printing

When you share a drawing or a cut sheet, the file is handed to your operating system's own share sheet or printer and goes wherever you send it. The app does not upload it and keeps no copy after you close the screen.

6. Ads and analytics

There are none. No advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no tracking pixel. This is deliberate: the app is meant to work in a basement with no signal, and a tool that phones home cannot promise that.

7. Children

Truemark is a professional trade tool. It is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, the Turkish KVKK and similar laws you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data. In practice: the app holds your data on your own device, so you already have all of it, and uninstalling erases it. For purchase records, contact Apple, Google or RevenueCat — they are the holders. If you want us to confirm any of this in writing, write to the address above.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published here before the app version that needs it ships.
