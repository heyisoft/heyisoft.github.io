2. What the app is

Truemark calculates conduit bends, run layouts, stair stringers and rafters, draws the result to scale and tells you where to mark the pipe. Every constant it uses is traceable to a published source, named on the screen that uses it.

3. What the app is not

  • Not an inspector and not a code authority. It does not check conduit fill, support spacing, bend radius limits, derating or anything else in the NEC or your local code. Those calls are yours.
  • Not a substitute for measuring. Take-up varies between benders, and a worn shoe is not the shoe in the table. The app tells you when a value came from a manufacturer sheet and when it came from your own measurement.
  • Not a guarantee. Geometry is exact; pipe, benders and buildings are not. Check the first bend before you cut the rest.

4. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the work you do with the numbers: verifying them against the job, following the code that applies where you are, and working safely. Do not use the app while driving, on a ladder, or anywhere that looking at a phone is not safe.

5. Free and Pro

Free, with no account and no ads: the Offset, Stub-Up and Kick calculators, with their full drawings, dimensions and mark rulers; every unit and precision setting; all nine languages; dark theme.

Truemark Pro additionally opens the other thirteen calculators, the digital level, saving and recalling jobs, sharing the drawing and cut sheet, and the step-by-step bending instructions.
