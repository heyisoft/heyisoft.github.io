Privacy Policy

🐾 The short version. Zerdava has no accounts. Your dog’s recordings, photos, diary and notes stay on your phone — the sound analysis runs entirely on the device and no audio is ever uploaded. The only thing that leaves your phone is anonymous usage and crash data, which never contains your dog’s name, sound, photo or notes. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

1. Who we are

Zerdava ("the app") is developed and published by heyisoft ("we", "us"). For any privacy matter, write to heyisoft@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Zerdava mobile application for iOS and Android, and this website. It explains what the app handles, where it is processed, and the choices you have.

4. What we do not collect

  • No audio ever leaves your phone. The sound model runs on the device; there is no server to send it to.
  • No photos, no notes, no dog names, no diary content.
  • No account, e-mail address, name or phone number — the app has no sign-up.
  • No contacts, files or precise location.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no ad networks.
  • No purchases or subscriptions — the app is free.

5. How the sound analysis works

When you record your dog, the app measures acoustic properties of the sound — duration, pitch, how the pitch moves, roughness, harmonic structure — and compares them against a model that ships inside the app. All of this happens on your phone, offline. Nothing is streamed, queued or uploaded. If you delete a recording, it is gone.

Zerdava is not a veterinary device and does not diagnose. Its "health radar" only points out when your dog’s own pattern shifts from its usual. If something feels off with your dog, talk to your vet.

6. Analytics and crash reporting

To keep improving the app we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They record anonymous events — for example that an analysis was attempted and whether it succeeded — together with basic technical data such as device model, operating-system version and app version. This is used in aggregate; it is not tied to your name or an account (there is none), it is never sold, and it never carries the content of your recordings, photos or notes. Both services are covered by Firebase's privacy terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone — required. It is the whole point of the app: recording your dog’s bark. Audio is analysed locally and never uploaded.
  • Camera — optional. Only to take a profile photo of your dog.
  • Photo library — optional. Only to pick a profile photo of your dog.

You can refuse or withdraw any of these in your system settings. Without the microphone the analysis cannot run, but the rest of the app still works.

8. Device backups

Because your data lives on the device, it may be included in the backup you control (Google One / iCloud), encrypted by Google or Apple. We never receive that backup. On Android we go a step further and exclude sound recordings and photos from cloud backup; they are still carried over on a direct phone-to-phone transfer, which does not pass through anyone's cloud.

9. Legal bases (GDPR / KVKK)

  • Performance of contract — running the app you installed and processing recordings on your device to give you the result you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — anonymous analytics and crash reporting to keep the app working and improve it.

10. Retention

  • Everything on your device stays until you delete it — per item, or all at once with Settings → Your data → Erase all my data — or until you uninstall the app.
  • Analytics and crash data are retained by Google under Firebase's own retention settings.

11. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and Turkish KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because essentially everything lives on your device, the most direct tool is the in-app Erase all my data action, which removes profiles, diary, chat, recordings and photos for good. For anything else, write to heyisoft@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

12. Children

Zerdava is a general-audience app about pets and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly process children's data.

13. Credits

The dog sounds bundled with the app are real field recordings, not synthesis. Every clip comes from Freesound: 34 are CC0 (public domain) and 16 are Creative Commons Attribution (BY 3.0 / BY 4.0). Attribution licences require credit, so the full clip-by-clip credit list — title, author, licence and a link back to the recording — ships inside the app, under Settings → Sound sources. We read each licence off its own source page rather than trusting a search filter, and we discarded every recording whose page carried any trace of another species. What we changed in each recording: a single vocalisation window was trimmed, everything below 80 Hz was filtered out, and the level was normalised.

The sound model was trained on the openly licensed subset of FSD50K — Fonseca, Favory, Pons, Font and Serra (IEEE/ACM TASLP, 2022) — restricted to clips released under CC0 or CC BY 3.0, plus additional CC0 Freesound recordings. Datasets carrying a NonCommercial clause were deliberately excluded.
