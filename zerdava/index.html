Zerdava

A dog sound reader that runs entirely on your phone. Record a bark, see what changed, keep a diary — and talk back with real recorded dog sounds. No ads, no subscription, and no audio ever leaves your device.

Listen

Record a bark and get the read: sound type, how worked up it sounds, pitch, roughness — with a confidence figure. When it isn’t sure, it says so instead of guessing.

Talk back

Tap a phrase and your dog hears a real recorded dog — a greeting yip, an insistent food whine, a contented yawn. Every clip is an openly licensed field recording (CC0 or CC BY), licence read off its own source page and credited inside the app. No synthesised beeps.

Diary

Every bark you keep becomes an entry. Over days you get a picture instead of a single guess.

Notices drift

Zerdava learns your dog’s normal and quietly flags when it shifts. It never names an illness and never diagnoses.

Learns your dog

Label a few of their sounds and the read sharpens for that individual. Dogs differ enormously — a chihuahua and a mastiff share almost no acoustics — so a general model only goes so far.

Stays on your phone

Recordings, photos and the diary never leave the device. One tap in Settings erases everything, for real.

🐾 Honest about what this is. Nobody can turn a bark into a sentence, and any app promising that is selling you something. Zerdava does not claim to read intent. It tells you the sound type — bark, growl, or a tonal call like a whine or howl — and how worked up your dog sounds. That is what the published research supports: when the same recordings are tested on dogs the model has never heard, context accuracy in the literature sits around 33–43%, barely above chance. Sound type is the part that holds up. Zerdava shows its confidence and stays quiet when the signal is weak. It is not a veterinary device and does not diagnose.
