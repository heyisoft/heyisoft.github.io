Before you write about a wrong result

It helps a lot if you tell us what the app said, what you think your dog meant, and roughly how the recording was made — how close the phone was, whether anything else was making noise. That is usually enough for us to reproduce it.

Deleting your data

Everything Zerdava knows lives on your phone. To remove it all at once, open Settings → Your data → Erase all my data in the app. That deletes dog profiles, the diary, chat history, recordings and photos. There is nothing on our side to delete, because nothing is uploaded — see the Privacy Policy.

Health questions

We cannot give veterinary advice, and Zerdava does not diagnose. If your dog seems unwell, please contact a veterinarian.
