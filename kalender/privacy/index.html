Privacy Policy

The short version. Kalender has no account and no server of its own. Your tasks and plans are stored only on your phone. Your calendar is read on the phone to plan around your meetings, and calendar data never leaves it.

Three things do leave the device: crash reports and usage statistics (through Google Firebase, never your task titles or calendar content, and you can turn both off in Settings), and subscription information: an anonymous identifier used to check whether Kalender Pro is active, plus the purchase record if you subscribe (through Apple or Google, and RevenueCat). There is no advertising and no advertising identifier.

1. Who we are

Kalender (“the app”) is developed and published by Heyisoft (“we”, “us”), based in Türkiye. Heyisoft is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). For any privacy matter, write to heyisoft@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Kalender app for iPhone and Android, and these web pages. It tells you what the app handles, where it is kept, what leaves your device and why, and the choices you have.

3. What stays on your phone

The following is stored in the app’s own storage on your device (a local SQLite database and files). We never receive it.

4. Your calendar

With your permission, Kalender reads your calendar (EventKit on iPhone, the Android calendar provider on Android) so it can plan around your meetings. It reads events from the calendars you include: title, start and end times, and whether you declined an event, so declined events are left out. It also reads the names of your calendars and their accounts so you can choose which ones to include.

  • Kalender never writes to or changes your calendar.
  • Events are read when they are needed and are not copied into the app’s database, its backups or its export file. Only the list of calendars you chose to include is saved.
  • Calendar data is never sent to us, to Firebase, to RevenueCat or to anyone else.
  • You can withdraw calendar access at any time in your phone’s settings. The app keeps working with your own tasks.

6. What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-up, name, email address or phone number.
  • No task content and no calendar content, ever.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID), and nothing is sold.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

7. Turning off crash reports and usage statistics

You can turn off crash reports and usage statistics with the switch in the app’s Settings. From then on the app sends neither. Deleting the app also stops all collection.

8. Legal bases (GDPR and KVKK)

  • Crash reports and usage statistics: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)). The data is minimal, not linked to your identity, and you can switch it off at any time.
  • Subscription status check: our legitimate interest in offering Kalender Pro and knowing whether it is active (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Purchases: performance of the contract you enter when you subscribe (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)), and legal obligations such as keeping transaction records (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).
  • Data that stays on your device, including your calendar, is processed by the app on your phone and is not received by us.

9. Service providers and transfers abroad

Google (Firebase) and RevenueCat process data on our behalf as service providers, and Apple and Google process payments under their own terms. Their servers may be located outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers (GDPR Chapter V, KVKK Art. 9), we rely on the standard contractual clauses these providers offer.

  • Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Play: policies.google.com/privacy

10. How long data is kept

  • On your device: tasks, plans and settings until you delete them or delete the app. Daily backups are kept for seven days and then replaced. “Delete all data” in Settings empties the app’s database but keeps the safety copy and the last seven daily backups on the phone so the step can be undone; deleting the app removes all of them.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: event-level data is kept for the shortest retention period Google Analytics offers (2 months). Reports built from it contain only totals.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal record-keeping duties.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Under KVKK Article 11 you have the right to learn whether your data is processed and why, to ask which third parties in Türkiye or abroad receive it, to have it corrected or deleted and those third parties told, to object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) in Türkiye.

To use any of these rights, write to heyisoft@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. Because we do not know your name or email and the data on our side is tied only to random identifiers, we may not be able to find a specific record; turning off the switch in Settings and deleting the app stops all further collection. Everything on your device is already under your control: you can export it, change it or delete it in the app.

12. Security

Data sent to Firebase, RevenueCat, Apple and Google travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On your phone, the app’s data sits in its private storage, protected by the operating system.

13. Children
