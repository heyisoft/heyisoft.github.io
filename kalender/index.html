Plans break. You don’t have to.

When your day falls apart, Kalender rebuilds it in one tap. Your meetings stay put, and you see what moves before it moves.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

  • Rebuild the day in one tap

    Tap “My day fell apart”, say how much energy you have left, and Kalender strings what is left of the day around your meetings.

  • See it before it moves

    Nothing moves without asking. You get a preview of what goes to tomorrow, and you can pull any task back.

  • Running late

    Stretch the task you are on and the ones after it shift along. Meetings do not.

  • Moved, never hidden

    Tasks that went to another day wait in a calm list you can always see.

  • Your calendar, read only

    Kalender reads your meetings to plan around them. It never changes an event, and calendar data never leaves your phone.

  • No account, your data on your phone

    Tasks live on the device, with a daily backup of the last seven days. You can export everything to a file at any time.
