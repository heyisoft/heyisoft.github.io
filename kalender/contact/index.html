Reporting a bug

Tell us what you tapped, what you expected and what happened instead. Your phone model, its operating system version and the app version help us find it faster. Please do not send us your export file unless we ask; it contains all your tasks.

Subscriptions

  • iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, then Kalender.
  • Android: Google Play Store, then your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. The Terms of Use explain how.

Your data

Your tasks and plans stay on your phone, so there is nothing on our side to send you or delete. You can export or delete them in the app’s Settings. For crash reports and usage statistics, turn off the switch in Settings. The Privacy Policy has the details.
