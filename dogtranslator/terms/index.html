A monthly subscription, Dog Translator Pro, removes these limits and opens the health radar. There is no free trial and there are no ads. 2. What the app is

Dog Translator records a dog vocalisation, measures its acoustic properties on your device, and shows which context that sound most resembles, together with a confidence figure. It also lets you keep a diary and play real recorded dog calls back to your dog. With the optional Dress up feature you can send a photo of your dog to be redrawn in a costume by an AI image model (Google Gemini).

3. What the app is not

  • Not a translator in the literal sense. No one can turn a bark into a sentence. The app maps acoustics onto contexts observed in research, and it tells you when it is unsure instead of guessing.
  • Not a veterinary or medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or rule out any condition. The "health radar" only flags shifts from your dog’s own usual pattern.
  • Not a substitute for a vet. If your dog seems unwell, contact a veterinarian. Never delay veterinary care because of something this app showed you.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Use the app on your own dog, considerately. Stop playing sounds if your dog seems stressed or agitated.
  • Do not use the app to record people without their knowledge.
  • Do not attempt to reverse-engineer, resell or redistribute the app or its bundled sound library, or to use the Dress up server other than through the app.

5. Free limits and subscription

Without a subscription, Dog Translator has these free limits:

  • Listening: your first 5 listens (recording and analysis) are free. Trying the bundled sample bark does not count.
  • Chat phrases: your first 5 chat phrases are free.
  • Diary: every listening result is saved to the diary automatically, and the diary is free for everyone.
  • Health radar: the health radar (voice changes and the weekly summary) is part of the subscription. It is available after your first recording; without the subscription, section titles and explanations stay readable, but the values, charts, weekly verdict and findings are blurred, and tapping them opens the subscription screen. The summary for your vet is part of the subscription. Your data is kept either way and the radar opens as soon as you subscribe.

Dog Translator Pro removes all of these limits: unlimited listening, every chat phrase and the health radar, for as long as the subscription is active. There is no free trial. It costs US$4.99 per month; the price in your own currency is shown in the app before you confirm. It also includes Dress up photo credits (section 6).

The subscription renews automatically every month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and your store account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours of that. The first payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. You manage or cancel it in your own store account — App Store → your name → Subscriptions on iOS, or Play Store → Payments & subscriptions on Android — not through us, and deleting the app does not cancel it. Payment is taken and refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. If a price changes you will be asked to agree before it applies to you.

If you reinstall or change phone, use Restore purchases on the unlock screen or in Settings to bring an active subscription back.

Dog Translator shows no ads of any kind. What the app does handle is set out in the Privacy Policy.

6. Photo credits

Each costume picture created with Dress up uses one photo credit. A credit is used only when a picture is actually delivered: if the photo is refused (for example, no dog is found, a person is visible or a safety filter blocks it) or something goes wrong, no credit is used. There are two kinds of credit:

  • Subscription credits. An active Dog Translator Pro subscription includes 20 credits per monthly billing period. Subscription credits are refilled at each renewal; credits left unused at the end of a period do not carry over, and they stop when the subscription ends.
  • Bought credits. A one-time pack of 20 photo credits costs US$6.99 (the price in your own currency is shown in the app before you confirm). Anyone can buy it, with or without a subscription. Bought credits never expire. If you also have subscription credits, those are used first.

Credits can only be used for Dress up in Dog Translator; they have no cash value and cannot be transferred or exchanged. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies; credits from a refunded purchase are removed from your balance. Because Dog Translator has no accounts, bought credits are linked to your store purchase and to the anonymous identifier of your install. If you reinstall or change phone, use Restore purchases; if your bought credits do not come back, write to heyisoft@gmail.com with the store order number and we will restore them.
