Terms of Use

The short version. You can record without an account and transcribe 300 minutes a month for free. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription through Apple that renews until you cancel it; the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You are responsible for having permission to record other people, and AI output can be wrong, so check what matters against the recording.

By installing or using the app you agree to these terms. The app is provided by Heyisoft. It is distributed through the Apple App Store, and Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies together with these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. What the app does

The app records audio, turns it into a transcript, and makes summaries and answers to your questions about it. Most of this happens on your device; the Privacy Policy explains what stays there and what can leave it.

3. Recording other people

When you record other people, getting their permission and following the law where you are is your responsibility. In some countries and states every participant must agree before a conversation is recorded, and recording without permission can be a criminal offence. Tell people before you record a meeting, interview or class. The app does not do this for you and does not give legal advice.

4. AI output can be wrong

Transcripts, speaker labels, summaries and answers are produced automatically and can be wrong, incomplete or misleading, especially with noise, distant microphones or several people speaking. Speaker identification is in beta. Before you rely on the output for anything important, such as legal, medical, financial or business decisions, check it against the recording.

5. Free and Pro

Free, with no account and no ads: recording, 300 minutes of transcription each month, a summary of your first recording, and exporting your notes. On devices that need cloud summaries, three recordings a month can be summarised for free.

Pro adds transcription beyond 300 minutes a month, summaries for every recording, Ask, translation, templates, speaker identification (beta), iCloud sync of your recordings and audio, and exporting several notes at once. On devices that need cloud summaries, Pro has a fair-use limit of 1,000 cloud requests a month and 100 a day, far above normal use. The in-app subscription screen always shows the current features and price before you subscribe.

When a subscription ends, you can still open and export everything you made.

The app checks your subscription status through RevenueCat, as the Privacy Policy describes.
