1. About Taslak

Taslak helps you prepare job applications. You can build and edit a CV in a template, check it against an applicant tracking system (ATS) style review, and track your applications. AI features can also create, improve, translate and proofread a CV, write cover letters and career e-mails, prepare interview questions and feedback, and compare your skills or job offers against a role.

Taslak is a writing assistant, not an employment decision tool. Scores and suggestions are AI-generated estimates meant to help you improve your documents. Taslak does not guarantee interviews, job offers or employment, or that any CV will pass a specific employer's applicant tracking system.
