Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 Version: 2.0 (Taslak 3.0)

This Privacy Policy describes how heyisoft ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Taslak mobile application (the "App") on iOS, iPadOS and Android.

Taslak works without an account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number to use the App. The CVs, letters, applications and notes you create are stored on your device, not on our servers. When you use an AI feature, the text needed for that request is sent to our server and to the AI model, processed, and returned to your device. This policy explains exactly what leaves your device, who receives it, and how long it is kept.

1. Who we are

Taslak is published by heyisoft, a software studio operated by Hüseyin İris, based in Türkiye. heyisoft is the controller of the personal data described in this policy for the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698).

You can contact us at any time at heyisoft@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Taslak App and to the backend service operated by heyisoft (the "Backend") that the App calls to run AI features and to produce PDF and Word files. It does not cover third-party websites or services you open from the App, or the App Store and Google Play, which have their own policies.

3. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the App's private storage on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it unless you send it with an AI request (section 4):

  • your CVs, profile, skills and target role;
  • ATS scores and their feedback;
  • cover letters, career e-mails and interview practice;
  • job applications you track, their stages, notes and interview times;
  • settings such as language, theme and onboarding answers.

Reminders. Interview and follow-up reminders are scheduled as local notifications by your device. They are not sent through any server, and you can turn them off in your device settings.

Backup. If you choose "Back up", the App creates a backup file and hands it to your phone's own save screen. You decide where it goes, for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or a local folder. The App never connects to your cloud account itself, and we never receive the backup. The file is not encrypted by the App, so store it somewhere you trust.

Sharing and printing. When you share, print or save a PDF or Word file, it goes to the app or printer you choose through your device's system share and print screens.

Taslak does not connect to LinkedIn, job boards or any employer system, and it does not submit applications on your behalf.

4. Data sent to our Backend and the AI model

When you use a feature that needs our server, the App sends only what that feature needs:

  • AI features (ATS score, creating, improving, translating or proofreading a CV, AI suggestions, cover letters, career e-mails, interview questions and feedback, skill gap, profile headline, offer comparison): the relevant CV text or the CV file you pick (PDF, DOC or DOCX, up to 5 MB), the job title, experience level and job description you enter, and any text you type for that feature, such as your interview answers.
  • PDF and Word files: to lay out your CV in a template, the CV content and, if you added one, your profile photo are sent to the Backend, which returns the finished file. The photo is used only for this and is not sent to the AI model.
  • Technical data sent with every request: an anonymous app user ID (a random ID created by RevenueCat, see section 5), your App language, and, after you watch a video ad, a flag saying the request was unlocked by an ad. Your IP address reaches the server as part of any internet connection.

The Backend extracts the text and sends it to Google Gemini, the AI model that writes and evaluates the content, then returns the result to your device. CV files, CV text, photos and AI results are processed in memory or in temporary files that are deleted as soon as the request finishes. We do not store your CV, your job descriptions or the AI output on our servers, and we do not use them to train AI models.

Your CV may contain sensitive details (for example health, religion or union membership). You choose what to include. Remove anything you do not want sent to the AI model before you use an AI feature.

5. Analytics, crash reports, purchases and ads

The App includes the following services from other companies:

Google Firebase Analytics

Records how the App is used, for example that a screen was opened, a CV was created or a score was finished, with details such as the score or the template used. It never receives your CV text, job descriptions or anything you type. Firebase also collects device and App data (model, OS version, App version, language, approximate country) and a random App instance ID. We use this to understand which features work and where people get stuck.

Google Firebase Crashlytics

When the App crashes or hits a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics sends us the crash trace, device model, operating system and App version, the device's memory and disk state at the time, and a random installation ID. This report is not linked to your identity, your CV or what you have written. We use it only to find and fix bugs.

RevenueCat

Manages Taslak Plus subscriptions. RevenueCat creates a random anonymous app user ID and receives your purchase and subscription status from the App Store or Google Play, along with device and App data such as country, currency and App version. Our Backend asks RevenueCat whether that ID has an active subscription before running subscriber-only AI features. RevenueCat does not receive your CV or your payment card details; payments are handled by Apple or Google.

Google AdMob

If you are not a subscriber, you can unlock some AI features by watching a rewarded video ad, served by Google AdMob. AdMob receives device and network data (such as IP address, device type and OS) and may use your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. On iPhone and iPad, the App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the first video. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized based on it. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Google settings. No ads are shown to subscribers, and ads never receive your CV or anything you type.

Meta (Facebook) SDK

We advertise Taslak on Meta platforms. The Meta SDK sends standard App events, such as App installs, App launches and purchases, together with device data, so that Meta can measure whether those ads work. On iPhone and iPad the advertising identifier is shared only if you allowed tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. The Meta SDK does not receive your CV or anything you type.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share your CV content with advertisers, employers or recruiters.

6. Why we use data (legal bases)

  • To provide the features you ask for, including AI processing, file creation and subscriptions: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Crash reports, product analytics, abuse prevention and cost limits: our legitimate interest in running a reliable, secure App (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Ad tracking with the advertising identifier on iOS: your consent through the App Tracking Transparency prompt (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Records required by law, if any: legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

Some of these providers process data outside Türkiye, the EEA and the UK, mainly in the United States. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and the safeguards required by Art. 9 of KVKK.

8. How long we keep data

  • CV files, CV text, photos, job descriptions and AI output: not stored on our servers. They are deleted when the request finishes.
  • Usage records: for each AI request the Backend records the time, the feature used, the AI model, the number of AI tokens used, its cost, and your anonymous app user ID. These records contain no CV content and no IP address. We keep them to track AI costs and enforce fair-use limits, and we delete them for a given ID on request (section 10).
  • Limit counters: request counts per IP address (for rate limiting) and daily AI and video-unlock counts per anonymous app user ID. They expire automatically after their time window, at most about one day.
  • Server logs: technical logs (time, feature, status, errors and the anonymous app user ID for limit and subscription checks) without CV content. They are overwritten automatically on a rolling basis.
  • Subscription status: cached on the Backend for about five minutes. RevenueCat keeps purchase history as long as needed to manage your subscription.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept by Firebase under the retention settings of our Firebase project (crash reports are kept for 90 days).
  • On-device data: until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.

9. Security

All communication between the App and the Backend uses HTTPS. Requests are protected by an app key and by rate limits. On your device, Taslak's data sits in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read and which is protected by your device's lock and storage encryption. No online service is completely secure, but we work to keep the risk low.

10. Your choices and rights

Because there is no account, most of your data is under your direct control:

  • Delete a CV, letter, e-mail, ATS score or application from its own menu in the App.
  • Delete all ATS scores at once with "Clear analysis history" in Profile.
  • Delete everything on the device by uninstalling the App. Backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.
  • Stop ad tracking on iOS in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or on Android in your Google ad settings.
  • Turn off reminders in your device's notification settings.

Subject to applicable law, including GDPR, UK GDPR and KVKK, you also have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Türkiye the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu; in the EU or UK your local supervisory authority).

The only data we hold about you on our servers is linked to the anonymous app user ID. To exercise a right, email heyisoft@gmail.com and we will help you find that ID. We reply within 30 days and may ask for information to verify the request.
