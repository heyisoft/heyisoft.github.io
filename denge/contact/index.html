Subscriptions

Denge Pro is billed by Apple. To change or cancel it, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Your data

Your photos, documents and everything the app works out from them stay on your iPhone, so there is nothing on our side to delete. Deleting the app removes its local data; albums it created in Photos stay until you delete them. The Privacy Policy has the details.
