Denge

Denge sorts your photo library on your iPhone, points out what you can let go of, and keeps the rest in albums that update themselves inside Apple Photos. Every photo is analysed on the device and never leaves your iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store. iPhone, iOS 18 or later.

  • Categories, made on the device

    Denge uses Apple Vision on your iPhone to sort the library into categories.

  • Cleanup

    It groups similar shots and finds blurry or dark photos, old screenshots, screen recordings and large videos. You decide what goes.

  • Review month by month

    Go through the library one month at a time and swipe to keep or delete.

  • Make room without deleting

    Shrink large videos, or turn Live Photos into stills.

  • Albums in Apple Photos

    Denge keeps albums that update themselves inside the Photos app, in a folder called Denge.

  • Documents

    The Documents tab sorts PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations and text files from folders you choose. The files stay where they are.

Denge is free to download. Denge Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription, billed by Apple. Read how the app handles your data in the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, or get in touch.
