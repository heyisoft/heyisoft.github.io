MakeDay

A day tracker that keeps its records on your phone. Log what you do in a tap, draw the lines you want to stay inside, and see what a week actually held — not what you meant it to hold.

A tap is the whole record

The eight things you log most sit on the home screen. One tap on water is a glass; one tap on coffee is a cup. Nothing else asks you a question.

Track anything

Water, sleep, workouts, coffee, cigarettes, screen time, spending, savings, deep work, reading, driving, mood — or something you name yourself. Counts, minutes, distances, money, ratings.

Limits and goals

Ten a day. Eight glasses. Four hours of deep work a week. MakeDay follows the line day by day, says something the moment you cross it, and celebrates when you reach it.

The week, read back

What you did, what you skipped, how many, how long, how much — by day, by week, by month, by year, against the week before.

Apple Health fills some of it in

Walks, sleep, workouts, mindful minutes, water, alcohol and mood can come straight from Apple Health, read-only, so you don't type what your phone already knows.

Your records stay yours

No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Everything lives in the app on your phone, and one tap exports the lot as CSV.

● What's free, and what isn't. Recording is free and stays free: every tracker, unlimited records, reminders, colours, Apple Health, CSV export, and limits on water and walking. MakeDay Premium is the analysis — limits and goals on every tracker, the whole report, and where each line stands today.
