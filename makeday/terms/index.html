2. What the app is

MakeDay records what you choose to record — how much water you drank, how long you worked, what you spent — keeps it on your device, follows the limits and goals you set, and reports it back to you by day, week, month and year. It can also read a small set of types from Apple Health, with your permission, so you do not have to type them twice.
