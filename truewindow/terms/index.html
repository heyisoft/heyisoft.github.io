Terms of Use

The short version. Truewindow calculates cutting lists, glass sizes and quotes for PVC and aluminium windows and doors from the measurements and system data you enter. It does the arithmetic; the job is still yours. Check the numbers against your own system data and make a trial piece before you cut the rest. Everything on screen is free; some outputs and your own profile systems are Truewindow Pro.

1. Agreement

By installing or using Truewindow ("the app") you agree to these terms. The app is provided by heyisoft. If you do not agree, please do not use it.

2. What the app is

Truewindow turns the measurements you enter into a drawing, a 3D view, profile cutting lists on stock bars, glass sizes, an accessory list and a material quote, and produces PDF documents (quote, workshop sheet, glass order, installation sheet) that you can share.

3. What the app is not

  • Not a substitute for checking. Cutting deductions, glass allowances, weld allowances and accessory rules depend on the profile system, the manufacturer's catalogue, your machines and your process. The app shows where each value comes from (your own entry, a source you named, or "not entered"). A value without a source is shown as "not computed", never guessed. Check your first piece before you cut the rest.
  • Not manufacturer data. The built-in systems are examples. They are not any manufacturer's catalogue and are not approved by one. Enter or copy your own system's values from its official catalogue.
  • Not a guarantee. Calculation is exact arithmetic on the values you give it; sheets, machines, sites and workshops are not.
  • Not an engineering or compliance check. The app does not verify structural strength, wind load, thermal or acoustic performance, or building codes.

You are responsible for the work you do with the results: verifying them against the job, following the standards and regulations that apply where you are, and working safely. Do not use the app anywhere that looking at a phone is not safe.

5. Free and Pro

Free, with no account and no ads: the model catalogue, the 3D view, entering measurements and seeing the drawing and 3D view change, one project, every unit setting and every theme.

Truewindow Pro opens everything that is calculated from your measurements: cutting lists, 6 m bar plans, glass sizes, deductions, hardware and material lists, totals, the quote, workshop, glass order and installation PDFs, customer mode, unlimited projects, extra quote items, discounts, payment tracking, your own and copied profile systems, and your logo on the quote.
