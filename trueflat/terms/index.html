2. What the app is

Trueflat develops the flat pattern of sheet-metal parts from the dimensions you enter, taking sheet thickness into account, and gives you the result as a drawing, a DXF file, a 1:1 PDF, a hand layout guide and a material quote.

3. What the app is not

  • Not a substitute for checking. Bend allowance, springback, kerf, weld gaps and seam allowances depend on your material, tools and process. Make and check the first part before you cut the rest.
  • Not a printer calibration. A 1:1 print is only 1:1 if your printer prints at 100%; measure the check line on every print.
  • Not a guarantee. Geometry is exact; sheets, machines and workshops are not.

Trueflat Pro opens your own dimensions, saving, DXF, PDF, the layout guide, the quote and sharing for every shape. DXF export is part of Pro on every shape, including the basic ones.
